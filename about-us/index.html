About Us

Making it simple to book lab tests and health checkups from the comfort of your home.

Who we are

Tata 1mg Labs helps you book the diagnostic tests you need without the hassle. Tell us what you’re looking for, and our team takes care of the rest — from choosing the right test to arranging sample collection.

What we do

  • Lab test booking assistance — help finding and booking the right lab test.
  • Home sample collection — samples collected from your doorstep by trained phlebotomists.
  • Health packages — preventive and full body checkups for different needs.
  • Radiology services — X-Ray, Ultrasound, CT Scan and MRI bookings.
  • The right team for your test — we connect you with the testing or service team best placed to help.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need — call +91-9650461818 or message us on WhatsApp. Our health advisors help you pick the right test or package and confirm any preparation, such as fasting.
  2. Pick a time slot — choose a time that suits you. A trained phlebotomist visits your home to collect the sample, with no extra charge for home collection.
  3. Testing at certified labs — your sample is transported to and processed at an accredited partner lab.
  4. Digital reports — reports are shared by email and WhatsApp. Routine tests such as CBC, Thyroid Profile and Lipid Profile are usually ready within 6 hours; tests such as Vitamin D and Vitamin B12 take around 24 hours.

Our partner labs and accreditations

Every sample booked through Tata 1mg Labs is processed at partner laboratories that hold recognised quality accreditations:

  • NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) — India's accreditation for medical laboratories under ISO 15189, covering testing methods, equipment calibration, staff competence and quality control.
  • CAP (College of American Pathologists) — an international accreditation based on regular inspections and proficiency testing of laboratory results.
  • ISO certification — internationally recognised standards for quality management and laboratory processes.

These accreditations mean the labs follow documented procedures, take part in external quality checks, and are audited regularly, so you can rely on the accuracy of your results.

Our commitment to you

  • Transparent pricing — the price you see is the price you pay, with home sample collection included. See our lab tests and health packages.
  • Privacy — your personal and health information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Help when you need it — our team is a call or WhatsApp message away before, during and after your test.
