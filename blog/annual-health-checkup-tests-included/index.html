Annual Health Checkup: Tests Included and How to Choose a Package

By Tata 1mg Labs · 21 November 2026 · 4 min read

An annual health checkup is a set of tests you repeat once a year, even when you feel well. Many common conditions, such as diabetes, high cholesterol, thyroid disorders and vitamin deficiencies, develop quietly for years before they cause symptoms. A yearly checkup catches these changes early and, just as importantly, builds a record of your results so you and your doctor can see how your health is changing over time.

Tests Included in an Annual Health Checkup

Most annual health checkup packages are built around the same core tests:

TestWhat it checks
CBC (Complete Blood Count)Anaemia, infection and platelet levels
Fasting Blood Sugar and HbA1cDiabetes and prediabetes
Lipid ProfileCholesterol and triglycerides, linked to heart risk
Liver Function Test (LFT)Fatty liver and liver stress
Kidney Function Test (KFT)How well your kidneys filter waste
Thyroid Profile (T3, T4, TSH)Underactive or overactive thyroid
Urine Routine & MicroscopyUrinary infection, kidney and sugar problems

More detailed packages add Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, iron studies, calcium and inflammation markers such as CRP. You can see every test, parameter by parameter, in our full body checkup test list with names.

What to Add at Each Age

  • 20s and 30s: the core tests above are usually enough for a yearly baseline. Add Vitamin D and B12 if you spend most of the day indoors or often feel tired.
  • 40s: add vitamin and iron studies, and consider heart risk markers if heart disease or diabetes runs in your family.
  • 50 and above: a comprehensive full body checkup with heart, kidney and inflammation markers gives a fuller picture. Your doctor may suggest checking some values more often than once a year.

How to Prepare

  • Fast for 10–12 hours before sample collection. Plain water is fine. See which blood tests need fasting.
  • Book an early-morning home collection slot so fasting is easy.
  • Avoid alcohol and heavy meals for a day before the test.
  • Keep last year's reports handy so you can compare the results.

Make It a Yearly Habit

Try to book your annual checkup in the same month every year, for example around your birthday or the start of the new year. Comparing the same tests year after year is what makes an annual health checkup valuable: a value that is slowly rising is often more important than a single result. If anything in your report is outside the normal range, a doctor consultation can help you understand what it means and what to do next.

Frequently Asked Questions

What tests are included in an annual health checkup?

Most annual health checkups include a CBC, fasting blood sugar and HbA1c, lipid profile, liver and kidney function tests, thyroid profile and a urine test. More detailed packages add Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, iron studies and inflammation markers.

How often should I get a health checkup?

For most healthy adults, once a year is a good routine. If you have an existing condition or a strong family history of disease, your doctor may suggest testing more often.

Do I need to fast for an annual health checkup?

Yes. Most packages include fasting blood sugar and a lipid profile, so fast for 10–12 hours before the sample is collected. Plain water is allowed.

Can I get an annual health checkup at home?

Yes. A trained phlebotomist collects your blood and urine samples at home at no extra charge, and reports are shared on email and WhatsApp.
