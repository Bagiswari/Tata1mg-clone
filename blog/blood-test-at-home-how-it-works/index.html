Blood Test at Home: How It Works, How to Prepare and When You Get Reports

By Tata 1mg Labs · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

A blood test at home gives you the same lab test you would get at a diagnostic centre, without the travel or the queue. A trained phlebotomist comes to your home, collects your sample in a few minutes, and the sample is tested at a certified lab. Here is how a blood test at home works, step by step.

How a Blood Test at Home Works

  1. Book your test. Choose a single test or a health package and book online, on a call or on WhatsApp. Home blood test booking takes a couple of minutes.
  2. Pick a time slot. Choose a time that suits you. For fasting tests, an early-morning slot is best.
  3. Sample collection at home. The phlebotomist confirms your details, collects a small blood sample from your arm and labels it. If your test needs a urine sample, you provide it in the container they bring.
  4. Testing at a certified lab. Your sample is taken to a NABL, CAP and ISO certified partner lab and processed there.
  5. Reports online. Your report is shared on email and WhatsApp. Routine tests such as CBC, thyroid profile and lipid profile are usually ready within 6–12 hours.

How to Prepare for a Blood Test at Home

  • Check if you need to fast. Fasting blood sugar and lipid profile need 10–12 hours of fasting; many other tests do not. See which blood tests need fasting.
  • Drink water. Plain water is allowed even when fasting and makes the blood draw easier.
  • Wear a loose sleeve that rolls up easily.
  • Keep your prescription handy if your doctor has asked for specific tests.
  • Tell the phlebotomist about your medicines, especially blood thinners, and keep taking regular medicines unless your doctor says otherwise.

Why Choose a Blood Test at Home?

  • No travel, no waiting room and no traffic.
  • Easier for elderly parents, people with limited mobility and busy professionals.
  • Fasting tests are simpler: give your sample first thing in the morning and have breakfast straight after.
  • Home sample collection is included at no extra charge, so the price you see is the price you pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a blood test at home work?

You book your test online, on a call or on WhatsApp and pick a time slot. A trained phlebotomist collects your blood sample at home, the sample is tested at a certified lab, and the report is shared on email and WhatsApp.

Is there an extra charge for a blood test at home?

No. Home sample collection is included at no extra charge. You only pay the price of the test or package.

How long does home sample collection take?

The blood draw itself usually takes just a few minutes once the phlebotomist arrives.

When will I get my blood test report?

Routine tests such as CBC, thyroid profile and lipid profile are usually ready within 6–12 hours. Specialised tests can take longer. Reports are shared on email and WhatsApp.
