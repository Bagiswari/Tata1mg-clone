Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a Tata 1mg blood test at home?

Call +91-9650461818 or message us on WhatsApp, choose your test or health package and pick a time slot. A trained phlebotomist will visit your home to collect the sample.

Is there any extra charge for home sample collection?

No. Home sample collection is included at no extra charge. You only pay the standard price of the test or package you book.

Which health packages are available?

Popular packages include the Good Health Gold Package (72 tests), Aayushya Health Checkup 2 (74 tests), Comprehensive Gold Full Body Checkup (86 tests), Comprehensive Platinum Full Body Checkup (100 tests) and Comprehensive Diamond Full Body Checkup (104 tests).

How soon will I get my lab test reports?

Reports for routine tests such as CBC, Thyroid Profile and Lipid Profile are usually ready within 6 hours, while tests such as Vitamin D and Vitamin B12 take around 24 hours. Reports are shared by email and WhatsApp.

Are samples tested at certified labs?

Yes. All samples are processed at NABL, CAP and ISO certified partner labs.

Which cities do you serve?

We offer home sample collection in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Ghaziabad, Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata and Lucknow.

Do I need to fast before a blood test?

Some tests, such as Fasting Blood Sugar and Lipid Profile, need fasting before sample collection. Our health advisors will confirm the preparation for your test when you book.
