Stress Impact Checkup for Women

The Stress Impact Checkup for Women is a comprehensive health assessment tailored to evaluate how stress affects various physiological functions and overall well-being in women. This checkup includes a range of blood tests to assess various metabolic, hormonal, and organ functions, aiming to identify stress-related health concerns.

  • Earliest reports in18 hours

What does Stress Impact Checkup for Women measure?

Package contains 46 tests

    • HbA1c (Glycosylated Hemoglobin)
  • Cortisol, Serum (Morning Sample)

  • Vitamin D (25-Hydroxy)
  • Vitamin B12

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Recommended Timings: Morning: 7-10 am.
  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.
  • In females, the preferred sampling time is day 2 or 3 of the menstrual cycle for LH & FSH.

Why is this package booked?

  • To evaluate overall health and identify potential issues exacerbated by chronic stress.
  • To early signs of stress-related health issues in women , including chronic fatigue, diabetes, or heart disease.
  • To detect signs of inflammation, or nutritional deficiencies associated with chronic stress.
  • To measure stress hormone (cortisol) levels and assess how well the body responds to stress.
  • To evaluate levels of key hormones to understand how stress is impacting reproductive health and overall hormonal balance.
  • To provide a comprehensive health overview affected by stress and guide tailored lifestyle, dietary, and medical interventions for improving health and managing stress effectively.
  • To motivate individuals to make informed decisions about their health and encourage positive lifestyle changes by focusing on areas of concern.
