Allergy & Food Intolerance

ImuPro Screen 22 (In collaboration with R.G.Biopharma, Germany)

The samples for ImuPro Screen 22 (In collaboration with R.G.Biopharma, Germany) are processed in collaboration with R.G. Biopharma, Germany. ImuPro Screen 22 (In collaboration with R.G.Biopharma, Germany) is a basic food intolerance test that checks IgG antibodies against 22 commonly eaten foods. It is done to help identify possible food-related sensitivities that may be linked to symptoms like bloating, stomach discomfort, skin problems, headaches, or fatigue. This test is useful if you have ongoing digestive or unexplained symptoms and want to understand whether certain foods may be contributing. It can help guide a supervised elimination diet and improve overall well-being. This test evaluates IgG responses only and does not replace IgE allergy testing. If you have a diagnosed IgE allergy, you must continue avoiding that food even if this test shows no reaction. IgE allergies can cause serious reactions. Always review results with a healthcare professional before making dietary changes.
