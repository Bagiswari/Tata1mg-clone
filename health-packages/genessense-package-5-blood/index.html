Genessense Package 5, Blood

Genessense Package 5 -Blood is a series of blood tests used to evaluate the genetic risks of various chronic diseases, genetic diseases, and individual responses to drugs. It comprises a KardioGen test, DiabetoGen test, VisiGen test, FitGen test, HyperSense test, NeuroSense test, OncoSense test, CureGen Advanced test, and CarrierGen test. It helps in determining the risk of developing chronic diseases such as heart disease; diabetes; age-related macular degeneration (AMD), a vision disorder; body mass index or body fat; high blood pressure; Parkinson’s & Alzheimer's disease; and various inherited cancers: disorders related to effects of medications; and diseases that could affect your child health. This package facilitates the identification of at-risk individuals before they develop this disease and eventually aids in its early intervention and a better disease management strategy. Note: Pre-test & post-test genetic counseling can be provided. If interested please send the request to this email id: care@1mg.com
