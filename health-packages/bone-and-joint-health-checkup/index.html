Bone & Joint Health Checkup

The Bone & Joint Health Checkup is a range of tests tailored to assess key markers of bone and joint health by evaluating your immune response, calcium and phosphorus levels, inflammation markers, and other important factors contributing to bone health. These tests help assess bone and joint strength, helping to detect early signs of joint disorders, bone diseases, or underlying health issues that impact bone and joint function.

  • Earliest reports in36 hours

Also known as

  • Bone profile blood tests

What does Bone & Joint Health Checkup measure?

Package contains 30 tests

  • Serum Calcium
  • ESR (Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate)
  • Anti Nuclear Antibody, EIA
  • CBC (Complete Blood Count) (21)
    • Differential Leukocyte Count (5)
    • Red Blood Cell Count
    • Hb (Hemoglobin)
    • Platelet Count
    • Total Leukocyte Count
    • Hematocrit
    • Mean Corpuscular Volume
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration
    • Mean Platelet Volume
    • PDW
    • RDW CV
    • Absolute Leucocyte Count (5)
  • CRP (C-Reactive Protein), Quantitative
  • Phosphorus, Serum
  • Rheumatoid Factor - Quantitative
  • Vitamin D (25-Hydroxy)
  • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)
  • Anti-CCP Antibody

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting is preferred but not mandatory. Follow the doctor's instructions regarding fasting requirements.

Why is this package booked?

  • To assess joint inflammation and bone density to detect signs of early joint disorders like arthritis or osteoarthritis.
  • To measure markers of inflammation and immune response to diagnose autoimmune disorders such as rheumatoid arthritis or lupus.
  • To monitor bone strength and metabolism, helping diagnose bone conditions like osteoporosis and osteomalacia.
  • To detect deficiencies in essential nutrients like calcium, vitamin D, and phosphorus that are crucial for bone health.
  • To identify potential bone infections, such as osteomyelitis, or metabolic conditions that could affect the skeletal system.
  • To assess joint and bone health for individuals undergoing treatments or surgeries that may impact the bones and joints.
