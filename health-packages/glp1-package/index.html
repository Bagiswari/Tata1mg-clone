Diabetes

GLP1 Package

The GLP1 Package is a comprehensive package designed to evaluate metabolic and hormonal factors responsible for weight gain (obesity), especially in individuals with type 2 diabetes. It evaluates metabolism, organ function, and vitamin levels to track health progress and detect risks during weight management. These tests help to understand how the body regulates insulin, metabolism, and energy balance, supporting personalized treatment plans with glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) based therapies that aid appetite control, blood sugar management, and weight loss.

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.

Why is this package booked?

  • To monitor health and safety in individuals undergoing GLP-1 therapy for weight loss.
  • To detect diabetes, prediabetes, or insulin resistance that could affect weight and metabolism.
  • To check lipid levels and assess cardiovascular health during weight management.
  • To evaluate liver and kidney function, ensuring safe metabolism and medication processing.
  • To monitor pancreatic health with enzyme tests (amylase, lipase).
  • To assess thyroid function, which influences energy, metabolism, and body weight.
  • To measure vitamin D and vitamin B12 levels, which are essential for bone health, nerve function, energy, and immunity.
  • To create personalized, safe, and effective weight management plans while reducing chronic disease risks.
