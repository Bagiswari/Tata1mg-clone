Chronic Liver Disease Screening Panel with FIB 4

The Chronic Liver Disease Screening Panel with FIB 4 is a comprehensive screening panel designed to assess liver health, identify the underlying causes of chronic liver disease (CLD), and evaluate the extent of liver damage. It includes tests that help detect common etiologies of CLD and assess liver function. The panel includes a Liver Function Test (LFT) to evaluate liver function and detect liver injury; Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), Hepatitis B core total antibody, and Hepatitis C virus (HCV) antibody to screen for chronic viral hepatitis; Anti-Nuclear Antibody (ANA) by IFA and Immunoglobulin G (IgG) to evaluate autoimmune liver diseases such as autoimmune hepatitis; Complete Blood Count (CBC) to identify blood cell abnormalities commonly associated with chronic liver disease; HbA1c and Lipid Profile to assess diabetes and metabolic risk factors associated with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD); Comprehensive Serum Iron Studies to detect iron overload disorders such as hereditary hemochromatosis; and Prothrombin Time (PT) with INR to assess the liver's ability to synthesize clotting factors and evaluate disease severity. FIB-4 Index is a scoring tool that uses age, AST, ALT, and platelet count to assess the risk of advanced liver fibrosis, particularly in individuals with chronic liver disease. Getting tested with the Chronic Liver Disease Screening Panel with FIB 4 enables early detection of chronic liver disease, helps identify its underlying cause, assesses disease severity, and supports timely diagnosis, appropriate treatment, and ongoing monitoring to reduce the risk of complications and disease progression.

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.
  • Avoid iron supplements for at least 24 hours prior to sample collection.
  • A duly filled coagulation requisition form (Annexure - CR/05) is mandatory for sample collection.
  • Kindly provide complete clinical details and medication history or doctor's prescription; this information is important for correlation of your test results.
