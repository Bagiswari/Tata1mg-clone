Who should take this package?

Adults over 40, people with a family history of heart disease, and anyone who wants allergy and pancreas markers checked as well. If you have symptoms or an existing condition, check with your doctor which tests you need.

Preparation

  • Fast for 10–12 hours before sample collection. Plain water is allowed.
  • Book an early-morning slot so the fasting window is easy to manage.
  • Avoid alcohol and heavy, fatty meals for 24 hours before the test.
  • Keep taking your regular medicines unless your doctor advises otherwise, and tell the phlebotomist what you take.

Report time

Reports are usually ready within 24–48 hours, as vitamin and specialised markers take longer to process than routine tests. Reports are shared by email and WhatsApp.

How home sample collection works

  1. Call +91-9650461818 or message us on WhatsApp to book the Comprehensive Platinum Full Body Checkup.
  2. Pick a time slot. A trained phlebotomist visits your home to collect the sample.
  3. Your sample is tested at a NABL, CAP and ISO certified partner lab.
  4. You receive your report by email and WhatsApp.
