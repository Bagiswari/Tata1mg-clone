Thalassemia Care Panel

The Thalassemia Care Panel is a set of blood tests tailored to track the health and manage the condition of individuals with thalassemia. These tests help assess important parameters such as iron levels, vital organ function, and overall health, allowing doctors to make informed decisions for long-term care and treatment plans effectively.

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • A duly filled Consent form for HIV testing & Pre-test counselling (Annexure - CR/01) is mandatory.
  • Avoid iron supplements for at least 24 hours prior to sample collection.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.

Why is this package booked?

  • To monitor iron levels and identify iron overload, which is common due to frequent blood transfusions.
  • To assess the function of vital organs such as the liver and kidneys, which may be affected by thalassemia or its treatment.
  • To track blood sugar and thyroid hormone levels, which can be influenced by the disease or its treatment.
  • To ensure early detection of complications, such as liver damage, kidney disease, or electrolyte imbalances.
  • To guide treatment plans and adjust iron chelation therapy, medications, or lifestyle changes as needed.
