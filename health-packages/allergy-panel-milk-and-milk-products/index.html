Allergy Panel - Milk & Milk Products

The Allergy Panel - Milk & Milk Products comprises a group of tests that measure specific immunoglobulin IgE and total IgE antibodies against various milk proteins, using the ImmunoCAP method. This panel helps identify potential milk-related allergies and helps doctors tailor targeted treatment plans..

  • Contains7 tests
  • Earliest reports in4 days
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Milk Allergy IgE Blood Test
  • Milk Allergy Test
  • Allergen Profile for Milk

What does Allergy Panel - Milk & Milk Products measure?

Package contains 7 tests

  • Total IgE
  • Allergen-Cow Milk (Doodh)
  • Allergy - Milk & Milk Product Panel
  • Allergen-Alpha -Lactalbumin (Milk protein)
  • Allergen-Beta - Lactoglobulin- Milk (Milk protein)
  • Allergen-Casein (Milk Protein / Dahi)
  • Allergen-Cheese Cheddar

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Clinical history is required.

Why is this package booked?

  • To confirm a milk allergy in individuals who experience allergic reactions after consuming milk and milk-based products.
  • To guide treatment plans, including dietary changes and medications.
  • To monitor the effectiveness of treatments and make adjustments if necessary.
  • To identify individuals at higher risk, especially those with a family history of food allergies or asthma.
