Children Health Checkup with Smart Report

The Children Health Checkup with Smart Report is a preventive health package specially designed for children to provide a broad overview of their overall health, growth, nutritional status, blood health, metabolism, and key organ functions. It brings together essential health checks that can help identify nutritional gaps or other health concerns early, supporting timely medical guidance and healthy development. The package includes a Complete Blood Count (CBC) test and Peripheral Smear Examination to assess different types of blood cells and provide insights into conditions such as anemia, infections, and other blood-related abnormalities. C-Reactive Protein (CRP), Quantitative test helps assess inflammation in the body. To evaluate metabolic health, the package includes a Lipid Profile, which can be done in a non-fasting state to measure cholesterol and triglyceride levels, along with HbA1c (Glycosylated Hemoglobin) to assess average blood sugar levels over the past few months. TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive test helps assess thyroid function, which plays an important role in a child's growth, development, and metabolism. The package also includes Kidney Function Test (KFT) with Electrolytes and Liver Function Test (LFT) to assess kidney and liver function and help evaluate the body's fluid and electrolyte balance. Because adequate nutrition is essential during the growing years, the package evaluates important nutrients through Serum Calcium, Phosphorus, Vitamin D (25-Hydroxy), Basic Iron Studies, Serum Ferritin, and Vitamin B12 tests. These tests provide useful insights into nutrients involved in healthy bones, blood formation, energy metabolism, and normal growth and development. Urine Routine & Microscopy (Urine R/M) is also included to assess urinary health and identify certain abnormalities. To make the results easier for parents to understand, Children Health Checkup with Smart Report includes an interactive smart health report with simple visuals, pictures, and infographics that present complex health parameters in an easy-to-understand format. Overall, Children Health Checkup with Smart Report provides a comprehensive snapshot of a child's health and nutritional well-being. Periodic preventive health screening, when appropriate for the child's age and individual health needs, can complement routine pediatric care and help parents and healthcare professionals make informed decisions to support healthy growth and development.

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.
  • UrineOur phlebotomist will provide a clean, sterile container for you to collect a urine sample.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting is preferred but not mandatory. Follow the doctor's instructions regarding fasting requirements.
  • Avoid iron supplements for at least 24 hours prior to sample collection.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.
  • The urine sample (a minimum quantity of 10 ml urine) must preferably be the first morning midstream urine (part of urine that comes after the first and before the last stream). Collect the urine sample in a sealed and sterile screw-capped container provided by our sample collection professional. Ensure that the urethral area (from where the urine is passed) is clean & container doesn't come in contact with your skin. You should submit all the required samples for this package at once during the scheduled sample collection.
