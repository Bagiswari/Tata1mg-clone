Pre Chemotherapy Package

The Pre Chemotherapy Package is a comprehensive evaluation done before starting chemotherapy. The package checks if the body is well prepared for the chemotherapy treatment and its potential effects.

  • Contains48 tests
  • Earliest reports in18 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Pre-Chemotherapy health checkup

What does Pre Chemotherapy Package measure?

Package contains 48 tests

  • Serum Calcium
  • Lactate Dehydrogenase, Serum
  • CBC (Complete Blood Count) (21)
    • Differential Leukocyte Count (5)
    • Red Blood Cell Count
    • Hb (Hemoglobin)
    • Platelet Count
    • Total Leukocyte Count
    • Hematocrit
    • Mean Corpuscular Volume
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration
    • Mean Platelet Volume
    • PDW
    • RDW CV
    • Absolute Leucocyte Count (5)
  • FBS (Fasting Blood Sugar)
  • Magnesium
  • PT INR (Prothrombin Time with INR)
  • Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time
  • HIV Combo (Antigen And Antibody) Test
  • LFT (Liver Function Test) (11)
    • Gamma Glutamyl Transferase
    • SGPT (Alanine Transaminase)
    • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)
    • SGOT (Aspartate Aminotransferase)
    • Protein Total, Serum (4)
    • Bilirubin (Total, Direct and Indirect) (3)
  • KFT with Electrolytes (Kidney Function Test with Electrolytes) (8)
    • Serum Creatinine
    • Uric Acid, Serum
    • Blood Urea Nitrogen
    • BUN/Creatinine Ratio
    • Blood Urea
    • Sodium
    • Chloride
    • Potassium
  • Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg)

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • A duly filled Consent form for HIV testing & Pre-test counselling (Annexure - CR/01) is mandatory.
  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • A duly filled coagulation requisition form (Annexure - CR/05) is mandatory for sample collection.
  • Kindly provide complete clinical details and medication history or doctor's prescription; this information is important for correlation of your test results.

Why is this package booked?

  • To assess overall health and organ function before starting chemotherapy.
  • To identify any underlying conditions that could affect the success of chemotherapy or recovery.
  • To measure vital markers such as blood counts, kidney function, and liver enzymes to ensure the body is ready for chemotherapy.
  • To look for infections like hepatitis B or HIV that could make treatment more complicated.
  • To check levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium, since imbalances can affect treatment or recovery.
  • To see if you have any blood clotting problems that could cause issues during chemotherapy.
  • To help doctors create a treatment plan that’s right for you, with the right dose and safety steps.
