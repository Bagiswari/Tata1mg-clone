Hypothyroidism / Hashimoto's Disease Panel

The Hypothyroidism / Hashimoto's Disease Panel is a specialized assessment tailored to provide a thorough evaluation of thyroid function and detect potential thyroid-related conditions such as hypothyroidism or hashimoto’s disease. It includes key tests such TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive, Anti-TPO Antibody (Anti-Thyroid Peroxidase Antibody) and Thyroxine (T4) Free, offering valuable insights into your thyroid health.

  • Contains3 tests
  • Earliest reports in18 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Autoimmune thyroiditis panel

What does Hypothyroidism / Hashimoto's Disease Panel measure?

Package contains 3 tests

  • TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive
  • Anti-TPO Antibody (Anti-Thyroid Peroxidase Antibody)
  • Thyroxine - Free (FT4)

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.

Why is this package booked?

  • When symptoms suggestive of thyroid dysfunction, such as persistent fatigue, unexplained weight gain, irregular menstrual cycles, or sensitivity to temperature, are present.
  • To screen for thyroid disorders in individuals with a family history of thyroid disease or autoimmune conditions.
  • To evaluate thyroid function in those undergoing treatments that can affect thyroid health.
  • To monitor treatment effectiveness in managing thyroid disorders and adjust therapeutic plans as necessary.
  • To assess thyroid health and adjust treatment in individuals with known thyroid disorders.
  • During pregnancy to identify potential risks related to thyroid disorders that may impact both the mother and the baby.
  • As part of an evaluation for infertility.
