Senior Citizen Essential Health Checkup with Smart Report

The Senior Citizen Essential Health Checkup with Smart Report is a comprehensive health assessment tailored to identify the age-related potential health problems in older adults. It includes a range of pathology tests to evaluate vital organs, nutritional status, and chronic disease markers. This package includes an easy-to-understand smart health report with tailored insights and recommendations for informed decision-making. Note: This package is scientifically curated for senior citizens (60 years and above). Individuals from other age groups may also book it if their test requirements or test parameters are similar.

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.
  • UrineOur phlebotomist will provide a clean, sterile container for you to collect a urine sample.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.
  • Avoid iron supplements for at least 24 hours prior to sample collection.
  • Collect a First morning/Random urine sample in a sealed and sterile screw-capped container provided by our sample collection professional. Women are advised not to give the sample during the menstrual period unless prescribed. Blood contaminated and highly turbid samples are not acceptable. (First morning sample is preferred)

Why is this package booked?

  • As part of a comprehensive screening to detect potential health risks common in older adults, such as diabetes, heart disease, rheumatoid arthritis, and osteoporosis.
  • To aid early identification and management of chronic conditions such as diabetes, liver or kidney diseases, thyroid disorders, etc.
  • To assess essential nutrients and joint health to prevent deficiencies and conditions like rheumatoid arthritis and osteoporosis.
  • To detect inflammation and assess immune function to identify underlying health conditions.
  • To provide insights into cardiovascular health and help manage risk factors for heart disease.
