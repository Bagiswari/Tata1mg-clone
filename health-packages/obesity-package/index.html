Obesity Package

The Obesity Package is a comprehensive health check designed to understand the underlying causes of weight gain. It helps develop a personalized plan to achieve sustainable weight loss. This package checks organ functioning and metabolic markers, providing insights into conditions like diabetes, cardiovascular diseases, and other vital organ diseases, all of which can contribute to obesity.

  • Contains55 tests
  • Earliest reports in18 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Obesity profile

What does Obesity Package measure?

Package contains 55 tests

  • CBC (Complete Blood Count) (21)
    • Differential Leukocyte Count (5)
    • Red Blood Cell Count
    • Hb (Hemoglobin)
    • Platelet Count
    • Total Leukocyte Count
    • Hematocrit
    • Mean Corpuscular Volume
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration
    • Absolute Leucocyte Count (5)
    • Mean Platelet Volume
    • PDW
    • RDW CV
  • HbA1c (Glycosylated Hemoglobin)
  • Thyroid Profile Total (T3, T4 & TSH) (3)
    • Thyroxine - Total (T4)
    • Triiodothyronine - Total (T3)
    • TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive
  • KFT with Electrolytes (Kidney Function Test with Electrolytes) (8)
    • Blood Urea Nitrogen
    • Serum Creatinine
    • BUN/Creatinine Ratio
    • Potassium
    • Blood Urea
    • Uric Acid, Serum
    • Chloride
    • Sodium
  • Vitamin B12
  • Lipid Profile (8)
    • Cholesterol - LDL
    • Triglycerides
    • Cholesterol - Total
    • Cholesterol - HDL
    • Very Low Density Lipoprotein
    • Total Cholesterol/HDL Cholesterol Ratio
    • LDL/HDL Ratio
    • Non HDL Cholesterol
  • FBS (Fasting Blood Sugar)
  • Vitamin D (25-Hydroxy)
  • LFT (Liver Function Test) (11)
    • Bilirubin (Total, Direct and Indirect) (3)
    • Gamma Glutamyl Transferase
    • SGPT (Alanine Transaminase)
    • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)
    • SGOT (Aspartate Aminotransferase)
    • Protein Total, Serum (4)

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.

Why is this package booked?

  • For the early detection of possible health risks and conditions related to obesity, such as diabetes, thyroid diseases, cardiovascular diseases, etc.
  • For a complete health assessment of metabolic or organ function to understand the overall health impact of obesity.
  • To monitor and manage risk factors before they lead to serious health issues.
  • To reduce the likelihood of developing obesity-related chronic diseases by addressing the underlying health problems.
  • To help doctors tailor recommendations for diet, exercise, and lifestyle modifications basis the individual health metrics.
  • To track the progress of health metrics to assess the effectiveness of implemented wellness strategies.
