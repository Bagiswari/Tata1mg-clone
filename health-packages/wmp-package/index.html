Lifestyle & Weight

WMP Package

The WMP Package, or weight management program, is a set of blood tests that help to assess metabolism, organ function, and vitamin levels. These tests help identify potential weight related health risks, providing insights for effective weight management.

  • Contains55 tests
  • Earliest reports in18 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Weight Management Checkup

What does WMP Package measure?

Package contains 55 tests

  • Vitamin B12
  • HbA1c (Glycosylated Hemoglobin)
  • CBC (Complete Blood Count) (21)
    • Differential Leukocyte Count (5)
    • Red Blood Cell Count
    • Hb (Hemoglobin)
    • Platelet Count
    • Total Leukocyte Count
    • Hematocrit
    • Mean Corpuscular Volume
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration
    • Mean Platelet Volume
    • PDW
    • RDW CV
    • Absolute Leucocyte Count (5)
  • FBS (Fasting Blood Sugar)
  • Lipid Profile (8)
    • Cholesterol - LDL
    • Triglycerides
    • Cholesterol - Total
    • Cholesterol - HDL
    • Very Low Density Lipoprotein
    • Total Cholesterol/HDL Cholesterol Ratio
    • LDL/HDL Ratio
    • Non HDL Cholesterol
  • Vitamin D (25-Hydroxy)
  • LFT (Liver Function Test) (11)
    • Gamma Glutamyl Transferase
    • SGPT (Alanine Transaminase)
    • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)
    • SGOT (Aspartate Aminotransferase)
    • Protein Total, Serum (4)
    • Bilirubin (Total, Direct and Indirect) (3)
  • Thyroid Profile Total (T3, T4 & TSH) (3)
    • Thyroxine - Total (T4)
    • Triiodothyronine - Total (T3)
    • TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive
  • KFT with Electrolytes (Kidney Function Test with Electrolytes) (8)
    • Serum Creatinine
    • Uric Acid, Serum
    • Blood Urea Nitrogen
    • BUN/Creatinine Ratio
    • Blood Urea
    • Sodium
    • Chloride
    • Potassium

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Overnight fasting (8-12 hrs) is required. Do not eat or drink anything except water before the test.
  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.

Why is this package booked?

  • To check overall health and find problems that could affect weight and general well-being.
  • To detect conditions like diabetes, prediabetes, and insulin resistance that can impact weight.
  • To measure lipid levels and assess the risk of heart disease.
  • To check liver and kidney function, which are important for metabolism and detoxification.
  • To detect hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism, which can significantly influence weight and metabolism.
  • To measure vitamin levels that are needed for nerve function, red blood cells, bone health, immunity, and energy.
  • To detect health issues early and create personalized plans for better weight management and health.
  • To reduce the risk of chronic diseases in people who are obese or underweight by addressing health issues early.
  • To encourage people to make better health choices and adopt positive lifestyle changes.
