Hyperthyroidism / Graves Disease Panel

The Hyperthyroidism / Graves Disease Panel test is a group of blood tests that supports the diagnosis and monitoring of hyperthyroidism (an overactive thyroid gland), especially in autoimmune thyroid disorders such as Graves’ disease.

  • Contains4 tests
  • Earliest reports in48 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Graves Disease Panel
  • Hyperthyroidism Panel

What does Hyperthyroidism / Graves Disease Panel measure?

Package contains 4 tests

  • Thyroid Profile Free (FT3, FT4 & TSH) (3)
    • Thyroxine - Free (FT4)
    • Triiodothyronine - Free (FT3)
    • TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive
  • TSH Receptor Antibodies

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.
  • High Biotin dose (>5 mg/day) should be avoided at least 8 hrs prior to blood collection.

Why is this package booked?

  • To evaluate thyroid hormone levels and assess overactive thyroid function (hyperthyroidism).
  • To help identify autoimmune activity associated with Graves’ disease.
  • To differentiate Graves’ disease from other causes of hyperthyroidism.
  • To assess the severity and activity of thyroid overactivity.
  • To support treatment planning and monitor response to therapy over time.
  • To aid clinical decision-making when interpreted along with symptoms and medical history.
