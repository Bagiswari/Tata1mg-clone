Allergy Panel - Asthma/Rhinitis, Comprehensive with Monitoring

The Allergy Panel - Asthma/Rhinitis, Comprehensive with Monitoring is a blood test that helps check for a wide range of substances that could trigger allergies, such as certain pollen, molds, animal dander, and dust mites. This helps pinpoint what is causing your asthma or rhinitis symptoms. Additionally the package includes Total IgE which reflects overall atopic tendency; Eosinophil Cationic Protein (ECP) which indicates eosinophil-driven inflammation and helps assess severity/monitoring of asthma; and Tryptase which helps detect mast cell activation, especially in suspected anaphylaxis or mast cell disorders.
