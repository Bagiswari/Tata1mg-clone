Covid Home Care Extended Package

The Covid Home Care Extended Package is tailored for individuals diagnosed with COVID-19 infection and managing their health and recovery in the comfort of their home. This comprehensive package includes an array of blood tests that evaluate key parameters such as immune function, organ health, and metabolic balance, providing crucial insights for ongoing infection and recovery health management.

  • Contains32 tests
  • Earliest reports in18 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Covid care at home

What does Covid Home Care Extended Package measure?

Package contains 32 tests

  • Lactate Dehydrogenase, Serum
  • CRP (C-Reactive Protein), Quantitative
  • Serum Creatinine
  • Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)
  • ESR (Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate)
  • Serum Albumin
  • D-Dimer
  • Serum Ferritin
  • SGOT (Aspartate Aminotransferase)
  • Complete Blood Count With NLR (22)
    • Differential Leukocyte Count (5)
    • Red Blood Cell Count
    • Hb (Hemoglobin)
    • Platelet Count
    • Total Leukocyte Count
    • Hematocrit
    • Mean Corpuscular Volume (MCV)
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration
    • Absolute Leucocyte Count (5)
    • Mean Platelet Volume
    • PDW
    • Neutrophil Lymphocyte Ratio
    • RDW CV
  • SGPT (Alanine Transaminase)

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • A duly filled coagulation requisition form (Annexure - CR/05) is mandatory for sample collection.
  • Overnight fasting is preferred but not mandatory. Follow the doctor's instructions regarding fasting requirements.
  • Kindly provide complete clinical details and medication history or doctor's prescription; this information is important for correlation of your test results.

Why is this package booked?

  • To maintain the health status of an individual diagnosed with COVID-19 from their own home without going to the hospital.
  • To monitor inflammation levels and organ functionality, which could be compromised during or post Covid-19 infection.
  • To assess and manage any consequences that may come after developing such issues, mainly blood clots or liver/kidney dysfunction.
  • To identify the possibilities of complications during home isolation or care.
  • For enabling healthcare professionals to make timely informed decisions for home care management.
