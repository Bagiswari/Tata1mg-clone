Thyroid Advanced Package

The Thyroid Advanced Package is a specialized assessment tailored to provide a thorough evaluation of thyroid function and detect potential thyroid-related conditions early. It includes key tests such as the Anti Thyroid Antibodies Panel and Thyroid Profile Total (T3, T4 & TSH), offering valuable insights into thyroid health.

  • Contains5 tests
  • Earliest reports in18 hours
  • Samples requiredBlood
  • ForMen & women

Also known as

  • Thyroid profile comprehensive
  • Premium Checkup for Thyroid Health

What does Thyroid Advanced Package measure?

Package contains 5 tests

  • Anti Thyroid Antibodies Panel (2)
    • Anti Thyroglobulin Antibody
    • Anti-TPO Antibody (Anti-Thyroid Peroxidase Antibody)
  • Thyroid Profile Total (T3, T4 & TSH) (3)
    • Thyroxine - Total (T4)
    • Triiodothyronine - Total (T3)
    • TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) Ultrasensitive

Samples required

  • BloodOur phlebotomist will draw a blood sample, typically from a vein in your inner elbow.

Preparations

  • Do not stop taking your thyroid medications on the day of the test unless otherwise advised by the doctor.

Why is this package booked?

  • As part of routine health check-ups to monitor thyroid function and overall well-being.
  • If you exhibit symptoms suggestive of thyroid dysfunction, such as fatigue, weight changes, irregular periods, mood swings, hair loss, etc.
  • To screen for thyroid disorders if you have risk factors like family history of thyroid disease or autoimmune conditions.
  • To monitor thyroid function in individuals undergoing treatments that can affect thyroid health, such as thyroid hormone replacement therapy.
  • To monitor the effectiveness of treatment for thyroid and autoimmune thyroid disorders.
  • To assess thyroid health and adjust treatment plans in individuals with known thyroid disorders.
  • During pregnancy to identify potential risks and complications related to thyroid disorders that may affect both the mother and the baby.
  • As a part of assessment of infertility.
